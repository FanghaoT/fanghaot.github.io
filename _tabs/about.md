I am Fanghao Tian, a curious and open-minded researcher exploring how nature and technology work. I am pursuing a PhD at KU Leuven and EnergyVille in Belgium. This blog is where I share practical engineering knowledge, work on AI and optimization, and occasional thoughts about life.

## Featured project

[OptimizationAlgorithm](https://github.com/FanghaoT/OptimizationAlgorithm) is my educational Python toolkit for heuristic optimization. It currently includes PSO, GA, Differential Evolution, Simulated Annealing, and NSGA-II, with shared APIs, reproducible examples, benchmark functions, and tests. Read the [project introduction]({% post_url 2026-08-01-From-PSO-to-NSGA-II-Building-an-Optimization-Algorithm-Toolkit %}) for an overview of the algorithms and multi-objective optimization workflow.
